Why We Use It

HTML5 is the structural foundation of every website we build. It is the skeleton that holds everything together. We use its modern semantic tags (like <header>, <article>, and <section>) to ensure your website is highly accessible and easily understood by search engines.

Before HTML5, playing videos or using complex graphics required clunky third-party plugins like Flash. Today, HTML5 natively supports audio, video, and scalable vector graphics (SVG), making the web faster, more secure, and mobile-friendly.

At Frost & Hog, we strictly write clean and standards-compliant HTML because a solid foundation is the first step to a secure digital infrastructure.
