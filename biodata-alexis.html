Alexis White

Co-Founder / Lead Web Dev

The Story

Alexis started her journey in the digital space as a self-taught graphic designer, fascinated by how typography and layout can influence human emotion. Before co-founding Frost & Hog, she worked across various independent agencies, shaping brand identities for tech startups and creative collectives.

At Frost & Hog, Alexis oversees all creative outputs. She believes that a strong digital presence shouldn't just look good—it must tell a compelling story. Her obsession with grid systems, dark mode aesthetics, and serif typography is what gives our agency its distinct "Editorial" edge.

When she's not pushing pixels or directing photoshoots, she spends her time curating independent film posters and exploring underground coffee shops.
